Banna Cherry Nut Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 stick of butter
  • 1 1/2 Cup sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 cups of flower
  • 1/4 Cup of milk
  • 1 cup crushed bananas
  • 1 Teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 cup Cherries
  • 1/2 cup walnuts

Procedure

Combine butter, sugar and eggs in a large dry bowl. Sift together dry ingredients. Alternate dry mixture, mashed bananas and milk into the wet batter, then add vanilla. Fold in cherries and nuts. Bake in Greased pan at 350deg for 45 minuets or until the cake is firm

Powdered Sugar Icing

Ingredients

  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of flower
  • 1 Cup of powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla or other extract
  • add milk until speadable

Procedure

Combine salt, flower, sugar and extract in a dry bowl. Add milk a little at a time until mixure is spreadable. Pour on top of Cake.
