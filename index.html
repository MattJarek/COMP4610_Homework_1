Banana Cherry Nut Bread with Powdered Sugar Icing

A link back to the github site.

This banana cherry nut bread is a moist, fruity twist on the classic banana bread. The cherries add a pop of sweetness, while the walnuts bring a satisfying crunch. It's topped off with a simple powdered sugar icing that ties it all together perfectly for breakfast, dessert, or an afternoon treat.

Activity Time
Preheat Oven 10 minutes
Prep and Mix Batter 25 minutes
Bake 45 minutes
Cool and Frost 30 minutes
Total Time 110 minutes

Banana Bread Ingredients

Most ingredients for banana bread are common in any household. The main components are

  • Flour
  • Baking Soda
  • Salt
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Bananas

Banana Cherry Nut Bread

The recipe for BCNB adds a few more ingredients compared to a simple Banana Bread to help the flavors, click here for an easy and printable list of ingredients to take with you to your next visit to the grocery store

Ingredients

  • 1 stick of butter
  • 1 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • 1 cup crushed bananas
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 cup cherries
  • 1/2 cup walnuts

Procedure

Combine butter, sugar and eggs in a large dry bowl. Sift together dry ingredients. Alternate dry mixture, mashed bananas and milk into the wet batter, then add vanilla. Fold in cherries and nuts. Bake in greased pan at 350°F for 45 minutes or until the cake is firm

Powdered Sugar Icing

Ingredients

  • 1/16 teaspoon salt (a pinch)
  • 1/16 teaspoon flour (a pinch)
  • 1 cup of powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla or other extract
  • 1-2 cups milk (add until spreadable)

Procedure

Combine salt, flour, sugar and extract in a dry bowl. Add milk a little at a time until mixture is spreadable. Pour on top of cake.
